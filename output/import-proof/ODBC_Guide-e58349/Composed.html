I N T RO D U C TI O NChapter 1: Introduction. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .Prior to the release of Document Production 9.0, Docucorp useddBase IV as the default DBMS for the Archive, WIP, and User Infotables. A flat file, called a Data File Definition (DFD), was used tospecify the layout of the database records and index files. Docucorpalso used these DFD files to specify the record layout of flat files(for example, batch files, the NewTrn file, and TrnFile). With therelease of Document Production 9.0, Docucorp introduced supportfor an ODBC connection to other databases such as Microsoft SQLServer and MySQL. This document explains how to set up the INIfiles to use this newer database interface technology.The steps to configure Document Production or Media View to usean ODBC database are:1Configure the INI files2Create the databases3Test the database connection4Migrate existing data©Copyright 2008 CSC.
