O F F S E T P RO C E S S I N GOverviewChapter 1: OFFSET Processing. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .Overview......................................................The original Document Production system only required a numericoffset value for its data retrieval processing. This was the offset intothe record where the data resided. You can still use numeric offsetsas before. However, if the data on the record was moved or otherdata was inserted before this data, then the numeric offset valueswould have to be changed to reflect the correct location of the data.This must be done everywhere the original offset value was used.This can result in maintenance issues.CSC has developed a methodology to externalize and isolate the off-set values for all of the possible records in the extract file. This iswhat we refer to as OFFSET processing. This methodology connects aspecific piece of extract file data to a variable field with minimal orno DDT mapping maintenance. The system administrator can make achange to the offsets file and have that change effect all DDT fieldsthat use that offset without having to edit any DDT file.Primary offset files are provided as part of Document Production’sbase system. The primary file contains the majority of offsets and isproduced through utility programs according to the host policy pro-cessing system. Secondary files consist of all hand-coded offsets andoffsets for records created during GENDATA execution.Definition......................................................The offset file contains a list of field names (column names) andtheir offset value into the record for all records that contain infor-mation needed by Document Production.
