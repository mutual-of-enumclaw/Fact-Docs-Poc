A.This endorsement provides only those coverages where a premium is shown in the schedule. It applies only to a covered "auto" described or designated in the Schedule.

B.For the covered “autos” described or designated in the Schedule above, the coverages shown in this endorsement replace coverages available under the Business Auto Enhancement Endorsement EA 99 10 Paragraph 4.a. under the changes to SECTION III – PHYSICAL DAMAGE COVERAGE.

C.If the Special Business Auto Enhancement Endorsement EA 99 11 is attached to the policy, coverages shown in this endorsement are considered primary for the covered “autos” described or designated in the Schedule above. Additional coverage provided in the Special Business Auto Enhancement Endorsement EA 99 11 Paragraph

4.a. under the changes to SECTION III – PHYSICAL DAMAGE COVERAGE is excess over coverage provided by this endorsement.

D.We will pay for rental reimbursement expenses for a rental “auto” or other transportation expenses incurred by you because of "loss" to a covered "auto”. Payment applies in addition to the applicable amount of coverage for "loss" to covered "auto”. No deductibles apply to this coverage.

E.We will pay only for those expenses incurred during the period beginning 24 hours after the "loss" and ending, regardless of the policy's expiration, with the lesser of the following number of days

1.The number of days reasonably required to repair or replace the covered "auto”. If "loss” is caused by theft, this number of days is added to the number of days it takes to locate the covered "auto" and return it to you.

2.The number of days shown in the Schedule.

F.Our payment is limited to the lesser of the following amounts:

1.Necessary and actual expenses incurred.

2.The maximum payment stated in the Schedule applicable to any one day or any one period

G.This coverage does not apply while there are spare or reserve "autos" available to you for your operations.

H.If “loss” results from the total theft of a covered “auto” of the private passenger type, and Business Auto Enhancement Endorsement EA 99 10 is on the policy, we will pay under this coverage only that amount of your rental reimbursement expenses which is not already provided for under Paragraph 4.c. of the Business Auto Enhancement Endorsement EA 99 10 under the changes to SECTION III – PHYSICAL DAMAGE COVERAGE.
