A.Premium Calculation Modification

1.The premiums that are shown in Item Three of the Declarations for liability, medical payments, uninsured motorist, underinsured motorist, and collision coverages, for the "autos" described in the schedule above were calculated including a credit for the "total lay up period" shown in the schedule above.

2.The "total lay-up period" for any "auto" must be at least 90 days for a credit to be included in the premium calculation for that "auto."

3.The "total lay up period" for any one "auto" may not exceed nine months in any policy term.

4.If you make a request in writing, we will change the number of months for the "total lay up period" for any "auto" during the policy term and adjust the premium calculation by endorsement.

5.The "total lay-up period" designated for any "auto" is part of the exposures that you told us that you would have and final premium calculation is subject to Paragraph 6. PREMIUM AUDIT of the Section B. GENERAL CONDITIONS of the Coverage Form.

A.Additional Definitions

"Lay-up" means any period of time during which an "auto" is parked, garaged or stored, and not in use for farm operations nor any other purpose. Occasional movement of an "auto" from one parking, garaging or storage location to another is not considered use.

"Total lay-up period" means the cumulative number of days in "lay-up" for any "auto" expressed as months.

1.Thirty days is considered one month.

2.If thirty does not evenly divide into the cumulative number of days, twenty or more remaining days shall be considered as a whole additional month.
