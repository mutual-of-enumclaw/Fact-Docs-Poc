            c.      Rental Reimbursement     Coverage   Extension
                (1)     We  will pay for necessary rental reimbursement expenses   incurred by you for the rental of an “auto”
                        because   of “loss” to a covered  “auto” for which  you  carry either Comprehensive    Coverage   or
                        Specified Causes  Of Loss  Coverage.  We  will pay only for those expenses incurred during the policy
                        period beginning 24  hours after the “loss” and ending, regardless of the policy's expiration, with the
                        lesser of the following number of days:
                    (a)     The number   of days reasonably required to repair or replace the covered “auto”;
                    (b)     The number   of days until we pay for its "loss”, or if “loss” is caused by theft, the number of days it
                            takes to locate the covered “auto”, return it to you, and, if necessary, make necessary repairs; or
                    (c)     The  number   of  days  shown  in  the Schedule   above  for Rental  Reimbursement    Coverage
                            Extension – Number   Of Days.
                    (2) This coverage  does  not apply  while there are spare  or reserve  “autos” available to you for your
                        operations.
                (3)     The  most  we  will under this Rental Reimbursement    Coverage   Extension  per day  is the limit of
                        insurance shown   in the Schedule above  for Rental Reimbursement   Coverage   Extension – Any  One
                        Day,  subject  to the  maximum    limit of insurance   shown   in the  Schedule   above  for  Rental
                        Reimbursement   Coverage   Extension – Any One  Period.
                (4)     If “loss” results from the total theft of a covered “auto”, we will pay under this Coverage Extension only
                        that amount  of your  rental reimbursement  expenses   which  is not already provided for under  the
                        Transportation Expenses  – All Covered Autos Broadened   Coverage  Extension.
                (5)     Coverage  under  this Coverage Extension  does not apply to rental reimbursement  expenses  covered
                        by the RENTAL   REIMBURSEMENT       COVERAGE     endorsement,   if attached to and made a part of this
                        policy.
                (6)     No deductibles apply to this coverage.
        J.      RETURN    OF  STOLEN   COVERED     AUTOS   EXPENSE    COVERAGE     EXTENSION
                The  following Coverage  Extension is added  to paragraph 4. Coverage   Extensions  as  found in paragraph A.
                Coverage   (SECTION   III – PHYSICAL  DAMAGE     COVERAGE):
            d.      Return  Of Stolen Covered   Autos  Expense  Coverage   Extension
                    We  will pay for reasonable, necessary,  and actual expenses   incurred to facilitate the return of a stolen
                    covered  “auto” to you. We will only pay under this Coverage Extension for those covered "autos" for which
                    you carry either Comprehensive  Coverage   or Specified Causes  Of Loss Coverage.  This coverage  will only
                    apply to vehicles recovered  inside the 48 contiguous United  States. This coverage  does not apply to an
                    “auto” we deem  a total “loss”.
                    Payments   provided under  this Coverage Extension  are included within, and do not increase, the limits of
                    insurance shown  in the Declarations for Comprehensive  Coverage   or Specified Causes  Of Loss Coverage
                    with respect to the stolen covered “auto”.
                    No deductible applies to this Coverage Extension.
